2. Sensor & Hardware Data Processing

FlipFold is designed specifically for foldable and flip Android devices. To provide fluid transition effects between device states, the Application accesses the following hardware APIs:

  • Hinge Angle Sensors: Measures the physical hinge fold angle in degrees via Android's hardware SensorManager.
  • Fold Posture & Window State: Determines whether your device is Flat, Half-Fold (Flex mode), or Closed via the Jetpack WindowManager API.
  • Display Configuration & Metrics: Reads screen resolution and orientation to align the visual transition overlay.

Sensor calculations and visual rendering run on-device. Local diagnostic traces can include fold angles, event timing and device information. These traces are not automatically uploaded. See the diagnostic report section below.

3. Personal Information & Permissions

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to use the Application.
  • No Location Data: The Application does not request or collect GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular location data.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The Application never requests access to your camera, audio, contacts, or calendar.
  • Screen Flow and Accessibility access: Before requesting screen access, FlipFold explains its use and asks for your agreement. If you enable the accessibility service, Screen Flow can take recent screenshots of the active display, including other apps, while effects are enabled, to prepare the page turn before folding. Images may contain information visible on screen. FlipFold does not analyse text, inspect the accessibility view hierarchy, or control other apps. It observes window-change and scroll event types and timing to refresh images. Screenshots remain in memory, are not included in diagnostic reports, and are never saved, shared or uploaded by FlipFold. Turn off effects to stop capture, or disable FlipFold screen access in Android Accessibility settings. Soft Shade and glass effects do not require this access.
  • Other permissions: Display-over-other-apps access lets FlipFold show temporary transition effects. A foreground service keeps fold detection running, and notification permission lets Android show its ongoing notification. Boot and app-update events can restore effects according to your saved settings and available permissions.
  • Local diagnostics: FlipFold maintains bounded local runtime diagnostics. When you record, save or export a phone report, it may contain device model and OS details, display and sensor information, fold angles and timing, permission and effect state, capture status, and performance or error information. It does not contain captured screen images. Reports are stored locally and sent only if you choose a recipient using Android’s share sheet. If you send a report to support, we use it to investigate your issue and retain it only as needed for that purpose.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Play handles installation, updates and lifetime purchases. Its billing service processes payment and account information under Google’s own policies. FlipFold does not receive your card details or Google account email through Play Billing.

FlipFold queries Google Play for product prices and purchase ownership. It verifies the signed purchase record and acknowledges completed purchases. A local record may include product and order identifiers, purchase time, purchase token and signature. The purchase token is used with Google Play for acknowledgement. A previously verified purchase can support offline access for up to 180 days after the last successful check; a successful ownership query applies refunded or revoked access immediately. Purchase date and order ID are shown in Settings when available.

7. Data Security & Storage

FlipFold does not upload captured screens or diagnostic traces to an app-operated backend. Settings, verified purchase records and saved reports are stored locally. Captured screen images are released from memory when no longer needed for an effect. The verified purchase cache is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Other app preferences may be backed up or restored by Android according to your system settings.

Uninstalling the app removes its private local data. Reports you exported to another folder or shared with another app remain there until you delete them. Google Play retains its purchase records independently; reinstalling FlipFold does not delete those records.
